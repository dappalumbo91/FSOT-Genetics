# Experimental disclosure (standard)

This software and its outcome cards are **experimental research**.

They are **not** a medical device, **not** a diagnosis, **not** a prescription, and **not** a substitute for a licensed clinician, a product label, or a clinical trial.

- Numbers are derived under pin `D1D38A` with **0 free parameters**, then **scored against public measured outcomes** (PDB co-crystals, FDA/CPIC-class facts, wet-lab mechanism papers) the same way Cα is scored against a crystal.
- Concordance with a known label is a **mechanism check**, not authorization to dose a patient.
- Discordance is a **diagnostic**: the wrong FSOT interface, missing data, or Superposed state — see `docs/OPEN.md` and the `diagnostic` field on each card.
- A field study and an intended-use lock are required before any clinical marketing claim.

If you are a patient, talk to your clinician. If you are a reviewer, use `data/experimental_pgx.json` as the frozen experimental scoreboard.
