FSOT α-HELIX HEPTAD COUPLING TRINARY MAP (F16)
============================================================
Same format as 64_codon_trinary_map.txt, 20-AA chemical map,
and beta_pair_trinary_map.txt. 2 trits = 9 cells.
Every coefficient closed-form in {π, e, φ, γ}.

Trit axes:
  H_i  Heptad face of residue i (i within helix A, local pos a = i - s_A)
  H_j  Heptad face of residue j (j within helix B, local pos b = j - s_B)

Heptad position trit definition (k = local pos mod 7):
  k ∈ {0, 3}    → +1  packing face (a, d — hydrophobic core, faces partner)
  k ∈ {4, 6}    →  0  ionic face   (e, g — salt-bridge edge)
  k ∈ {1, 2, 5} → -1  outward face (b, c, f — solvent-exposed)

Multiplier rule:
  m(H_i, H_j) = φ ^ (H_i + H_j)

DERIVATION
------------------------------------------------------------
The α-helix has 3.6 residues per turn. A coiled-coil pattern of two
packed helices uses a heptad repeat (7 residues = 2 turns) labeled
a-b-c-d-e-f-g. Positions a and d are on the same side of the helix
and form the hydrophobic core when two helices pack. Positions e and g
are on the edges and form inter-helix salt bridges. Positions b, c, f
face the solvent.

For any pair of residues (i in helix A, j in helix B), the geometric
likelihood of direct contact is determined by which face each residue
sits on. The (+1, +1) packing-packing case is the dominant interaction
(φ²), the (0, 0) ionic-ionic is neutral (1), and the (-1, -1) outward-
outward is forbidden (1/φ²).

WHY φ^(H_i + H_j)
------------------------------------------------------------
The two trits are independent — each residue's face is set by its own
position mod 7. The product φ^(H_i+H_j) is the natural FSOT golden-mean
multiplicative encoding. Diagonal symmetry (M(a,b) = M(b,a)) is automatic.

INTEGRATION POINT
------------------------------------------------------------
Used as multiplicative gate inside F13 (region-pair contact) when both
regions are Helix kind. Replaces uniform multiplier = 1.0 with the
heptad-localized signal. Source of truth:
